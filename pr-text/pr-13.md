## Description

Frames and frame embeds can be sized:

- **by zoom**
- **by width:** page, full or a percentage
- **by a locked aspect ratio**

They can also hide the background grid, inner frames and notes.

Also fixes connector ends missing an off-grid anchor while snap-to-grid is on.

![Demo](https://spmp.github.io/affine-pr-docs/assets/images/pr-13/hero.webp)
