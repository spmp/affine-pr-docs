## Description

- **Mind-map shapes** (central idea, branch, sub-topic, square). A mind-map
  tree collapses to its root.
- **An add-child panel** for mind-map shapes, with palette colours by
  children or by depth (configurable in settings).
- **Container shapes** (container, vertical, horizontal) that collapse their
  contents.
