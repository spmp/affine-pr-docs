<!-- Title: feat(frame): embed the frame in exported PNGs and restore it on import -->
A frame exported as PNG carries its content in PNG metadata. Pasting or
dropping that PNG back onto the canvas recreates the **editable** frame.
Ordinary PNGs are unaffected.

Details: https://spmp.github.io/affine-pr-notes/pr-14/
