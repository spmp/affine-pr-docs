## Description

A frame exported as PNG carries its content in PNG metadata. Inserting that
PNG again, on a page or the canvas, offers to **convert it back to an
editable frame** ("Convert to Frame" / "Keep Image"). Ordinary PNGs are
unaffected.

Frames and frame embeds also get Export/Import Frame PNG and Frame Metadata
in their menus.

![Demo](https://spmp.github.io/affine-pr-docs/assets/images/pr-14/hero.webp)

Full write-up and testing notes: https://spmp.github.io/affine-pr-docs/pr-14/
