## Description

Adds **Flip horizontal** and **Flip vertical** for shapes.

- Text inside a flipped shape stays readable.
- Attached connectors follow the mirrored anchors.
- Rotation of a flipped shape goes the right way.

![Demo](https://spmp.github.io/affine-pr-docs/assets/images/pr-07/hero.webp)
