Adds the draw.io stencil libraries to the shape browser: **about 8,900
shapes in about 200 categories,** including AWS, GCP, Azure, Cisco and
Kubernetes. They have thumbnails and search.

Stencil data is **lazy-loaded**, so it adds nothing to the entry chunk or first
load.

**Reviewer note:** almost all of the +5.6 M lines are generated data from
`scripts/drawio-library-manager.mjs`. Please review the generator and the
loading code. The licence of the draw.io stencils needs confirming. Happy to
move the data to static assets or a separate package if preferred.

Details: https://spmp.github.io/affine-pr-notes/pr-10/
