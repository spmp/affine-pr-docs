Adds a Workbox service worker to the web and mobile apps:

- **Offline launch.** The app shell is precached, so the app opens offline,
  including from the iOS home screen.
- **Lazy chunks** are cached as they are used.
- **Updates** are prompted, so the app never reloads under the user.
- **Proper manifest icons.**

Navigations are routed before the precache, so the home-screen launch URL
(`/?source=pwa`) works offline.

Tested on iPhone in airplane mode.

Details: https://spmp.github.io/affine-pr-docs/pr-41/
