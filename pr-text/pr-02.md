<!-- Title: feat(edgeless): colour palettes and gradient fills for shapes, pen and connectors -->
Adds **palette sets** to the edgeless shape, pen and connector tools:

- matched line and fill swatches
- gradient fills
- palettes managed in *Settings → Appearance → Palettes*, stored per workspace

Built-in palettes: AFFiNE, Material light, Material gradient and Bold.

Each basic shape keeps its own last-used style.

Screenshots and details: https://spmp.github.io/affine-pr-notes/pr-02/
