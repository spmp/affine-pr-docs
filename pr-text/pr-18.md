Show a **note** or a **table** from this page or another page in place,
**live and editable.** Edits write back to the source.

- **Inserting:** `/Note: <name>`, `/New note`, `/Table: <name>` and
  `/Reference` (any doc).
- **Note references** have:
  - a name, background and border
  - Move to page…
  - full editing: slash menu, undo, paste, and the caret on iOS
- **Deleting is safe.** Permanently deleting a page rescues referenced blocks
  into a page that references them.
- **Search** indexes note, frame and table names for the picker. Also fixes
  docs that went permanently missing from search when their title was
  missing; the indexer version is bumped.

Screenshots, design notes and the nested-editor details: https://spmp.github.io/affine-pr-notes/pr-18/
