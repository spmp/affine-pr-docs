## Why

A frame exported as PNG is a dead image. Shared by chat or email and later
dropped back into AFFiNE, it can't be edited. draw.io solves this by putting
the diagram inside the PNG. This PR does the same for AFFiNE frames.

## What changes

- **Exported PNGs carry the frame.** Exporting a frame as PNG embeds its
  content (the frame, its elements and their connectors) as metadata in the
  file.
- **Insert an image, convert it to a frame.** When an inserted image
  (uploaded, dropped or pasted) carries frame metadata, AFFiNE asks
  *"Frame metadata detected: convert this image to an editable Frame?"* with
  **Convert to Frame** or **Keep Image**:
  - **On a page,** the image becomes an embed of the recreated frame.
  - **On the edgeless canvas,** the frame is recreated where the image was,
    and the image is removed.

  An ordinary PNG is inserted as an image, with no prompt.
- **Frame menu actions.** The `…` menu of a frame, and the toolbar of a frame
  embed on a page, gain **Export Frame PNG**, **Export Frame Metadata**,
  **Import Frame PNG** and **Import Frame Metadata**. The import actions
  replace the frame's content with the content from the file.
- **Copy and paste keeps connectors attached.** Pasted connectors are created
  after the shapes they join, so they reconnect to the pasted copies, and
  their waypoints move with the paste.

## How it works

- `shared/src/utils/png-metadata.ts` reads and writes PNG text chunks
  (`embedPngMetadata`, `extractPngMetadata`), with its own CRC32.
- `blocks/frame/src/metadata.ts` serialises a frame's content.
- `clipboard/create-elements.ts` recreates it.
- `blocks/image/src/utils.ts` (`offerFrameConversion`) checks every image
  inserted through `addImages`, `addImageBlocks` and `addSiblingImageBlocks`
  for frame metadata and shows the convert prompt. This was planned as a
  separate `pr/16-frame-from-image-insert`. It landed here instead, so pr/16
  has no changes and will be dropped.
- The frame `…` menu actions are in `blocks/frame/src/frame-toolbar.ts`; the
  frame-embed ones in `blocks/surface-ref/src/configs/toolbar.ts`.
- HTML coming from metadata is sanitised before use.
