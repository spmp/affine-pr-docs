## Why

A frame exported as PNG is a dead image. Shared by chat or email and later
dropped back into AFFiNE, it can't be edited. draw.io solves this by putting
the diagram inside the PNG. This PR does the same for AFFiNE frames.

## What changes

- **Exported PNGs carry the frame.** Exporting a frame as PNG embeds its
  content (the frame, its elements and their connectors) as metadata in the
  file.
- **Pasting or dropping such a PNG** onto the edgeless canvas recreates the
  editable frame, not an image. An ordinary PNG is still inserted as an image.

## How it works

- `shared/src/utils/png-metadata.ts` reads and writes PNG text chunks
  (`embedPngMetadata`, `extractPngMetadata`), with its own CRC32.
- `blocks/frame/src/metadata.ts` serialises a frame's content.
- `clipboard/create-elements.ts` recreates it.
- The edgeless clipboard and image upload paths check incoming PNGs for frame
  metadata.
- HTML coming from metadata is sanitised before use.
