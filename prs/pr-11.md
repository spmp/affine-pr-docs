# pr/11: Collapsible containers and mind-map shapes

## Why

Large diagrams need to be folded away: a container whose contents can be
hidden, or a tree of connected ideas that can be collapsed to its root.

## What changes

- **Collapsible containers.** Container shapes from [pr/09](../pr-09/) get a
  collapse button. Collapsing hides every element inside the container.
- **Collapsible connector trees.** A shape at the root of a connector tree,
  such as a mind-map idea, can collapse all its descendants.
- **A mind-map shape panel.** From a selected mind-map shape, add a connected
  child shape in a chosen direction.
- **Mind-map palette.** New children cycle through the mind-map palette.
- **Mind-map branch shape** for drawing branches.
- **Settings.** Mind-map shape defaults are in the edgeless shape settings.

<!-- IMAGE (GIF): collapsing and expanding a container and a mind-map tree -->

## How it works

- `gfx/shape/src/element-view.ts` adds the collapse button and the hide and
  show logic for container contents and connector descendants.
- The collapse button's z-index follows its shape.
- `mindmap-shape-panel.ts` in `edgeless-selected-rect` is the add-child panel.
