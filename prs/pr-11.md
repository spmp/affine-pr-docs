## Why

Large diagrams need to be folded away: a container whose contents can be
hidden, or a tree of connected ideas that can be collapsed to its root.

## What changes

- **Mind-map shapes:** central idea, branch, sub-topic and square, in a new
  **Advanced** group in the shape browser.
  - **Collapsible.** A mind-map shape at the root of a connector tree can
    collapse all its descendants, and expand them again.
  - **Add-child panel.** From a selected mind-map shape, add a connected
    child shape in a chosen direction.
  - **Branch colours.** New children take colours from the mind-map palette.
- **Container shapes:** container, vertical container and horizontal
  container, also in the Advanced group. A container has a title bar and a
  collapse button; collapsing it hides every element inside it.
- **Settings** (Settings → Editor → Edgeless → Shape):
  - **Mindmap next color:** how a new child's colour is chosen: by
    **Children** (each sibling gets the next colour), by **Depth** (each level
    gets the next colour), or **Disabled**.
  - **Mindmap palette size:** how many palette colours to cycle through
    (1–11).

## How it works

- `gfx/shape/src/element-view.ts` adds the collapse button and the hide and
  show logic for container contents and connector descendants.
- The collapse button's z-index follows its shape.
- `mindmap-shape-panel.ts` in `edgeless-selected-rect` is the add-child panel.
