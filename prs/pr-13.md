## Why

When a frame is shown in a page as a frame embed (surface reference), its
size and what it shows are fixed. A slide-like frame needs a fixed aspect
ratio. An embedded diagram may need to fill the page width, or be shown at a
set zoom. The background grid and nested frames are often noise in the
embedded view.

## What changes

- **Sizing modes for frames and frame embeds:**
  - **zoom:** a fixed scale
  - **width:** page width, full width, or a percentage
  - **aspect lock:** a chosen aspect ratio, such as 16:9 or 4:3
- **Render options.** Show or hide the background grid, inner frames and notes.
- **Where to set them.** In the frame toolbar, the frame-embed toolbar, and the
  advanced properties panel.
- **Connector fix.** Dragging a connector end now snaps to a nearby anchor even
  when snap-to-grid is on and the anchor is not on a grid point. Before, the
  grid snap always won.

## How it works

- `FrameBlockProps` and `SurfaceRefProps` gain:
  - `frameScaleMode` and `frameZoomScale`
  - `frameWidthMode` and `frameWidthScale`
  - `frameAspectLock` and `frameAspectRatio`
  - `frameRenderOptions` (`showGrid`, `showInnerFrames`, `showNotes`)
  - `pageSize*` for the embed
- `surface-ref-block.ts` applies them when rendering.
- `resolveConnectorEndpointTarget()` in `connector-handle.ts` tries the raw
  cursor position for an anchor first, and grid-snaps only when no anchor is
  found.
