# pr/07: Flip shapes horizontally and vertically

## Why

Asymmetric shapes such as triangles, arrows and callouts often need to be
mirrored. Rotation alone can't do it.

## What changes

- **Flip horizontal and Flip vertical** in the edgeless "more" menu for
  shapes.
- **Text stays readable.** The text inside a flipped shape is not mirrored.
- **Connectors follow.** Connectors attached to a flipped shape stay attached
  to the mirrored anchor positions.
- **Rotation.** Rotating a flipped shape turns it in the direction you drag.

## How it works

- `ShapeElementModel` gains `flipX` and `flipY`.
- The shape renderers (canvas and DOM) apply the flip to the shape path only,
  not the text.
- `connector-anchor-utils.ts` mirrors anchor positions.
- The resize manager in `std/gfx/interactivity` reverses the rotation delta
  for a shape flipped on one axis only.
