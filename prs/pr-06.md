# pr/06: Start a connector by hovering a shape

## Why

Today you draw a connector by picking the connector tool first, or by
dragging from a selected shape's auto-complete handles. Diagram tools usually
let you hover a shape, see its connection points, and drag straight out of
one.

## What changes

- **Anchors on hover.** Hovering a shape shows its edge anchors (connection
  points).
- **Drag from an anchor.** Clicking or dragging from an anchor starts a
  connector from that exact point.
- **Only the shape under the pointer** shows anchors.
- **Notes still take input.** Hovering doesn't block typing into an edgeless
  note.
- **Anchors move with the shape.**
- **A setting.** *Settings → Editor → Connector → Hover to initiate* turns
  this behaviour on or off.

<!-- IMAGE (GIF): hover a shape, anchors appear, drag a connector from one -->

## How it works

- A new widget, `blocksuite/affine/widgets/edgeless-connector-anchors`, draws
  the anchors for the hovered element and hands the drag to the connector tool
  (`connector-tool.ts`).
- It is registered in `affine/all` view extensions.
