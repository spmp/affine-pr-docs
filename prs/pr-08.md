## Why

Neat diagrams need elements that line up. The edgeless background grid is
decorative only: its size is fixed, and nothing snaps to it.

## What changes

- **A grid menu in the zoom toolbar:**
  - **Grid size:** the background grid spacing.
  - **Snap:** snap moving and resizing elements to the grid.
  - **Grid snap anchor:** which point of an element snaps. Choose from 9
    points: the corners, the edge centres or the centre.
- **Guides and the grid.** Alignment guides don't snap when snapping is off,
  or while resizing.
- **Connectors.** Connectors stay draggable with snapping on, and keep their
  rotation after a flip.
- **PNG export** draws the grid at the custom size (it was fixed at 20 px).
- **Scribbled shapes** draw the dotted stroke from [pr/03](../pr-03/).

## How it works

- The grid menu is `widgets/edgeless-zoom-toolbar/src/grid-menu.ts`. Its
  settings live in `EditPropsStore`.
- Snapping is in `gfx/pointer/src/snap/snap-overlay.ts`.
- `getBgGridGap` takes the grid size.
- Connector routing points respect the grid in `connector-routing-points.ts`.
