## Why

Edgeless lines are solid, dashed or none. A dotted line is a common diagram
convention, for example for optional flows or boundaries, and it is missing.

## What changes

- **A dotted (`Dot`) stroke style** in the line-style panel, alongside solid,
  dash and none.
- **Where it applies:**
  - shapes: rectangle, ellipse, diamond and triangle, in both General and
    Scribbled styles
  - connectors
  - edgeless note borders
- **Settings match the toolbar.** The edgeless settings for shapes,
  connectors and notes offer the same border styles as the toolbar.
- **Finer line widths.** The width steps in the panel are finer.

<!-- IMAGE: line-style panel with the Dot option, and a dotted connector between two shapes -->

## How it works

- `StrokeStyle.Dot` already exists in the model. This PR adds it to
  `line-styles-panel.ts` and renders it:
  - the shape renderers use a dot dash pattern
  - the connector DOM and canvas renderers draw it
  - `edgeless-note-background.ts` draws it for note borders
- The settings pages (`connector.tsx`, `note.tsx`, `shape.tsx`) list the same
  options.
